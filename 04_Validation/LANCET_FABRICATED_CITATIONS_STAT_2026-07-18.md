## Key figure

- Quarterly rate of fabricated references per 10,000 papers in PubMed Central from January 2023 to February 2026.
- Stable at approximately **4 per 10,000 papers** throughout 2023.
- Rose sharply beginning mid-2024.
- Reached approximately **57 per 10,000 papers** by early 2026.

## Audit scope

- 2,471,758 papers scanned.
- 125,615,773 structured references extracted.
- 97.1 million references (77%) carried a PMID and were verified.
- 4,046 fabricated references identified across 2,810 papers.
- Precision of automated verification system: 91% (Fleiss' κ = 0.71).
