[CLAIM]
On 2025-06-12 the claimant purchased an "Audio Pro Gen 2"
loudspeaker from an Australian retailer for A$599. The retail
packaging and the manufacturer's product page displayed the
following representations:

  (a) "Maximum SPL: 106 dB at 1 m"
  (b) "Suitable for small-to-medium indoor venues (50-150 m^2)"

The claimant used the product in a 60 m^2 living room at typical
listening levels. Independent acoustic measurement (calibrated
Brüel & Kjær 2240 sound level meter, microphone at the listening
position, 1 m from the speaker face, pink noise input) showed a
maximum sustained SPL of 94 dB. The 106 dB figure was achievable
only with a continuous sine-wave input at 1 kHz, which is not
how the product is used in practice.

The 12 dB shortfall between the advertised and measured SPL
materially affects the product's fitness for the advertised
purpose. The claimant seeks a refund under the consumer
guarantees and ACL s 29.
