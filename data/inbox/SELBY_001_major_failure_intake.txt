[CLAIM]
On 27 August 2025 the claimant paid A$625.00 by card (card ending
X907, transaction reference AUD0000000062500, processed 29 Aug
2025) at Selby Acoustics Malvern for one Audio Pro C10 MkII "W"
speaker. On 6 December 2025 the claimant paid a second A$625.00
by Osko transfer (acknowledgment 780712659, recipient Centennial
Marketing Pty Ltd, BSB 193-879 / account 483-074-513, description
"C 10 MKII W") for a second unit, under Tax Invoice #25-00064092
issued by salesperson Hamish K.

The invoice and payment description explicitly identified the
product as "Audio Pro C10 MKII Wireless Multiroom Speaker White"
/ "C 10 MKII W" / "C10 MKII-WH" (system ID 142376, barcode
7330117152013). The "W" designation refers to the 2025
W-generation (Generation 3) Audio Pro C10 MkII, which features
DSP retuning for a 10-15 dB increase in usable loudness, WiiM/
Linkplay integration for stable multiroom sync, and compatibility
with the A28 W and SW-10 expansion units.

On both occasions the respondent physically supplied the 2021
Addon C10 MkII (Generation 2) legacy hardware instead. The
respondent's staff admitted in writing (email enquiry ticket
#422580) that they were "clearing remaining Gen 2 stock" and
that "AudioPro's W range still isn't available in Australia."

The supplied Generation 2 hardware lacks the DSP retuning, the
WiiM/Linkplay integration, and the A28 W / SW-10 timing
architecture. It is materially unfit for the disclosed purpose
of a researched music-centric multiroom system. The A$625.00
unit price aligns with the 2025 W-generation market valuation
(A$590-A$749); the supplied Gen 2 units retail at A$400-A$500.
The documented overpayment is approximately A$125-A$225 per unit
(A$250-A$450 total).

The claimant seeks a full refund of A$1,250.00 under ACL s 259(3)
on the basis of a Major Failure under ACL s 260, together with
misleading or deceptive conduct under ACL s 18 and failure of
goods to match description under ACL s 56.
