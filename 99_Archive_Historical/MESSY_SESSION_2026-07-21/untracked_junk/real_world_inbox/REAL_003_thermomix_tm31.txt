REAL 003  --  VORWERK THERMOMIX TM31 (REAL-WORLD CASE)
Source:        Federal Court of Australia, ACCC v Vorwerk International AG
               (Thermomix TM31)
Public record: Federal Court judgment December 2018; ACCC media
               release; CHOICE investigation.
Outcome (real): Thermomix fined A$4.6 million by the Federal Court for
                misleading consumers about the safety of the TM31.

[CLAIMANT]
Australian Competition and Consumer Commission (regulator)
on behalf of affected TM31 owners.

[CLAIM]
Vorwerk (the Thermomix manufacturer/distributor) represented to
consumers, and to regulators via its public statements, that:
  - the TM31 was safe to use when, in fact, the locking blade/sealing
    ring mechanism had a known failure mode that could cause the
    mixing bowl to open under pressure;
  - consumer injuries were isolated incidents attributable to user
    error rather than a product defect;
  - the replacement sealing ring issued in November 2014 fully
    addressed the issue, when injuries continued to be reported after
    that date.

In fact, the Federal Court found Thermomix made misleading
representations about the safety of the TM31 by representing the
product as safe when it knew of the failure mode and the injury
reports.

[REPRESENTATIONS FOUND BY THE COURT TO BE MISLEADING]
- Thermomix public statement: "The Thermomix TM31 is safe to use when
  operated in accordance with the instructions."
- Thermomix statement to CHOICE/media: "Reports of incidents are
  isolated and are the result of misuse by consumers."
- Thermomix statement: "The issue has been fully resolved by the
  November 2014 replacement sealing ring."
- These representations were made while Thermomix was aware of a
  significant number of injury reports, including burns, occurring
  both before and after the November 2014 replacement.

[PRODUCT FACTS]
- Product: Thermomix TM31 (kitchen multi-cooker, sold 2004-2014).
- Price: approximately A$2,089 per unit (Australian RRP).
- Units sold in Australia: approximately 200,000+ TM31 units.
- Injury reports: 87 cases of incidents reported to the consumer
  watchdog (CHOICE/ACCC), including burns requiring medical
  treatment.
- Failure mode: the sealing ring / locking blade mechanism could
  fail, causing the heated mixing bowl to open and eject hot
  contents under pressure.
- Federal Court penalty: A$4.6 million (December 2018).

[REQUESTED]
SUSTAINED. The ACCC sought (and obtained):
(a) Declarations that Thermomix made misleading representations
    in breach of ACL s 18 and s 29 (safety representations).
(b) A penalty of A$4.6 million (ordered by the Federal Court,
    December 2018).
(c) A corrective publication.

[JURISDICTION]
- Australian Consumer Law, sections 18 (misleading or deceptive
  conduct), 29 (false or misleading representations about the
  safety of goods), 54 (guarantees as to acceptable quality).
- The case established that representing a product as safe while
  aware of a known failure mode and injury reports is misleading
  conduct even if some incidents involve user error.
