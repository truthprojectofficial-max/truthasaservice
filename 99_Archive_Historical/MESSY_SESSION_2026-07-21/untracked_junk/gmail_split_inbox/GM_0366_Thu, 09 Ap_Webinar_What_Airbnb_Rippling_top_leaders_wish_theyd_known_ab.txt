Most AI programs don't fail in the pilot. They fail after it. 
Adoption stalls. Governance bottlenecks growth. The mandate from leadership 
doesn't translate into actual change on the ground. And the leaders who've been 
through it rarely say so in public.

 On April 23rd, they will.

 AI Leaders Lab — Zapier's invite-only executive community for AI 
transformation leaders — is opening to the public for the first time. Brandon

working, what broke, and what they'd do differently. The panel includes:

 → Iain Roberts, Chief People Officer, Airbnb
 → Hannah Calhoon, VP of AI Innovation, Indeed
 → Ankur Bhatt, VP of AI, Rippling

 It’s free and the recording is for registered attendees only.
