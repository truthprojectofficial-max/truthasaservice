Dear believers; sisters and brothers in Christ,

I know you all as those who live and move and have their being in Christ
and it is a joy and privilege to be walking alongside you for such a time
as this.  As we know, by the grace the God, the veil has been torn, that we
might go in and bring our prayers and supplication to Him who is faith,
knowing that he hears us and is faithful to give to those who believe.
Life on earth is not always easy for anyone, not even for us who believe,
but we have received power from above and authority in Jesus' name to
trample on serpents and scorpions and over all the power of the enemy. And
nothing shall by any means hurt us.

My prayer for you all, is that you be strengthen with might through His
Spirit in the inner man, that Christ may dwell in your hearts through
faith; that you being rooted and grounded in  love may be able to
comprehend with all the saints what is the width and length and depth and
height— to know the love of Christ which passes knowledge; that you may be
filled with all the fullness of God. (Eph 3:16-19)

We know that what this world needs is more faith, hope and love and so may
it be that we, by His grace through faith, go into the world and fill it
with His glory.

Love
Karen
