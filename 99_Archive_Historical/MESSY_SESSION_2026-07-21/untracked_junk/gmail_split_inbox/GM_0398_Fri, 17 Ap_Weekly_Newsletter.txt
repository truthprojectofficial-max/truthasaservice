**Dear Learners,**
------------------


🎊 You’ve made it through the week - happy Friday!


There may be times during your studies when motivation feels low or progress feels slow. During these moments, it can make a big different to reconnect with your “why” - the reason you chose your course or career path.


Your “why” is personal. It might be building a better future, changing careers, gaining new skills, or creating more opportunities for yourself and your family.


When you stay connected to your purpose, it becomes easier to stay focused and keep moving forward, even when things feel challenging.

**Knowing your “why” can help you:**


- Push through periods of low motivation 
- Stay aligned with what you’re working towards 
- Strengthen resilience through challenges 
- Clarify your priorities and focus your time effectively 
- Trust yourself and your progress 

Take a moment to reflect on what motivated you to start your course. You might write it down, set it as a reminder, or revisit it whenever you need a boost. 🤔💭


🧩 Remember, your goals are unique to you and every step you take is bringing you closer to achieving them.


**Hope you have a great week ahead!**

###**Job Opportunities**

####**1. Life Support Health Care (Melbourne, VIC)**


Life Support Health Care ([https://www.lifesupporthealthcare.com.au/](https://www.lifesupporthealthcare.com.au/)) are looking for Disability Support Workers in the south East suburbs of Melbourne (around Clayton,  Oakleigh, Mount Waverley,  Ashwood, Springvale, Noble park, Dandenong).


They are looking for compassionate and dedicated Disability Support Workers to assist their clients with:


- ✅ Personal care 
- ✅ Home duties assistance 
- ✅ Community participation 
If you are interested, please email copies of the following documents to [admin@lifesupporthealthcare.com.au](mailto:admin@lifesupporthealthcare.com.au) to apply:


- 📌 Resume 
- 📌 Certificate in Disability or individual support 
- 📌 Certificate in First Aid 
- 📌 Certificate in CPR 
- 📌 Driver’s License or Passport 
- 📌 Valid Working Status Document 
- 📌 NDIS Screening or Police Check 


####**2. Care Support Workers (Adelaide, SA)**
The Barossa Hills Fleurieu Local Health Network has multiple casual roles for Community Support Workers (Community Aged Care) and is seeking applicants who have, or are working towards, a relevant Certificate 3 level qualification.


These roles suit compassionate and motivated people to provide direct, personalised care to aged, frail, or disabled individuals who wish to remain living independently in their own homes. Working under a Consumer Directed Care model, you will support clients with personal care, meal preparation, cleaning, transport, and social engagement, enabling them to maintain control over their care and lifestyle choices. 


These roles require flexibility, empathy, and the ability to work autonomously while collaborating with clients, their families, and other health professionals. It offers a rewarding opportunity to make a meaningful difference in the lives of community members by promoting wellbeing, independence, and quality of life in a supportive and professional environment. 


**Barossa Hills Fleurieu Local Health Network requires people who:**


- ✅ Are compassionate and enjoy working with vulnerable people 
- ✅ Can build effective working relationships and communicate with care with a wide range of diverse people including their residents 
- ✅ Thrives on being organised and takes safety seriously 
- ✅ Like to engage with people from diverse backgrounds and with complex needs 
- ✅ Have a passion to support others to achieve exceptional patient centred care 

Relocation financial support for the roles may be negotiated, subject to eligibility. 


For more information or to apply, you can access the job posting on South Australia Health’s website via: [https://careers.sahealth.sa.gov.au/caw/en/job/932680?lApplicationSubSourceID=&nbsp](https://careers.sahealth.sa.gov.au/caw/en/job/932680?lApplicationSubSourceID= );
