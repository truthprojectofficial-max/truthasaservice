**Don’t Be Afraid to Ask for Help**
===================================

**Finding support helps you to succeed**
----------------------------------------

**THIS WEEK AT **
-----------------

**A GLANCE**
------------


- Asking for help is important for managing increasing work, study, and personal commitments 
- Seeking support encourages growth, learning, and stronger relationships 
- Remember our training team is here to help with your studies

**Dear Learners,**
------------------


Well done making it through another week of work and study!


It’s getting to that time of year again when we are becoming busier and busier with work, study and family commitments and many of us may need extra support to stay on track and achieve our goals.


Asking for the help we need is not always easy. Most of us like to do things on our own and be independent, but seeking assistance from others can also be a form of self-care.


Asking for support can often take you out of your comfort zone and show that you are working on something that is new.  This is helping you to grow, develop, and learn. Recognising when things may be too much to take on alone is important. Seeking support can provide new ideas, perspectives, and insights, while also helping you build stronger relationships with those around you.


Remember, that when it comes to your study, our training team is always ready and willing to support you (remember that you can find ACCSC’s contact details are always at the bottom of the newsletter). Don’t apologise or feel silly asking your trainer for assistance – that is what we are here for.


Please remember when asking for help from your trainer, being specific about what you would like assistance with is key to getting the help you need as quickly as possible. 


**Make sure you tell your trainer: **


- The **unit code** you are working on e.g., CHCDIV001 
- Whether you are completing **Assessment 1 or 2**, completing your **Placement **requirements, or need clarity about something in the** Learner Guide ** 
- The **question number(s)** you would like help with; and/or the **specific section number** in the Learner Guide

It’s always appreciated when you thank your trainer for their support, they will also see the impact of their guidance when you submit your completed assessment.


When asking for help in a situation or at work or home you will usually be able to approach the person directly.  With online training, think of an email or phone call as being the same as putting up your hand in a classroom to ask a question.


**Happy Friday! We look forward to helping you soon.**
