                                          Important updates to your Everyday Mastercard®    Dear Cardholder,
                                                                                       
                                            
We’ve now rolled out the previously advised important updates to your Australia Post Everyday Mastercard. These changes are designed to give you simpler, smarter and safer ways to use your card and manage your money. This email confirms what has changed and what it means for you.
Foreign Currency Wallets have been retired
Foreign currency wallets are no longer available on the Australia Post Everyday Mastercard.
If you had a foreign currency balance on your card after the cut-off date of 22 April 2026 11:59pm AEST, your remaining foreign currency funds have been automatically converted to AUD, using the exchange rate applicable at the time of conversion. The rates are set in accordance with the PDS. The rates are updated daily.
What this means for you:•You can now load and hold funds in AUD only•You can continue to shop instore or online at millions of locations worldwide where Mastercard is accepted^•When you spend in another currency, your AUD balance will automatically be converted at the time of purchase•Your card continues to work as normal with no interruption
If you have any questions, click here for FAQ’s or contact our card services team on 1800 549 718.
Added protection for peace of mind
Your Everyday Mastercard continues to include Mastercard Zero Liability Protection*, so you’re not liable for unauthorised transactions.
Effective today your card also includes complimentary Purchase Protection#.
Purchase Protection# provides up to 180 consecutive days of free worldwide cover against theft and accidental damage over a wide range of new personal goods purchased anywhere in the world, provided the purchase is charged to the account holder’s Everyday Mastercard. Limits and terms & conditions apply#
You do not need to enrol for this benefit; it is automatically activated when you make your first eligible purchase with your Everyday Mastercard.
For more information about Purchase Protection click here
Reloads via BPAY have been removed
>From today, BPAY is no longer available as a load option as we focus on more immediate load methods. You can continue to reload online via ‘My Account’, using the Australia Post Everyday Card App or by bank transfer.  You can also reload your card at any participating Australia Post Outlets using cash or a debit card.
If you’ve been using BPAY to load your card, we suggest removing any saved BPAY details from your internet banking as these payments will no longer be processed.

App Update and Google Pay
Your Everyday Mastercard now includes new digital features via Google Pay‡
Important: Update Your Details
To ensure a smooth experience, please ensure your mobile number is up to date and in the correct format: +6104XXXXXXXX.  You can update your phone number or email address in ‘My Account’ online or via the Mobile App. This will give you uninterrupted access to new features.
