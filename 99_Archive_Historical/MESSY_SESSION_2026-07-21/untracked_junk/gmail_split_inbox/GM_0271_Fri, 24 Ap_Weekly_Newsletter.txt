**Stay safe in the workplace**
==============================

**28th April is World Day for Safety and Health at Work **
----------------------------------------------------------

**THIS WEEK AT **
-----------------

**A GLANCE**
------------


- World Day for Safety and Health at Work is on Tuesday 28 April and promotes safer, healthier workplaces 
- Workplace safety is a shared responsibility between workers and employers 
- Following safety procedures and reporting hazards helps prevent injury and illness

**Dear Learners,**
------------------


World Day for Safety and Health at Work is next Tuesday, the 28th of April. This is an annual day that promotes the prevention of occupational hazards and diseases in the workplace. This day is all about raising international awareness of how to create a safe and healthy work culture. 


If you are working or on placement, you are responsible for protecting yourself in the workplace by knowing your own rights regarding safety. Similarly, it is your employer’s responsibility to help you manage a safe and healthy work environment.


Knowing how to work safely is important as it can help you reduce the risk of work-related deaths or injuries. 

**Here are a few tips on how to stay safe at work:**


- **Follow instructions on how to use tools and equipment properly:** always wear the required safety gear when handling heavy equipment and machinery 
- **Report any unsafe conditions to your employer:** employers should have policies in place that clearly outline the process the company will follow to manage and report work, health, and safety 
- **Take a break:** if the nature of the work you are doing is straining, then make sure to take regular or longer breaks in between work hours 
- **Be aware of your surroundings:** clean up any spills, or remove items on the floor that people could trip on 
- **Remain aware of safety procedures:** ask your employer about policies you are unsure of - it is your responsibility to understand safety policies and to protect yourself 

Research has shown that managing a healthy and safe work environment has many benefits, including increased productivity and better employee morale. Here at ACCSC, we are committed to creating a safe and healthy workspace for all our workers and students. 


Consider taking these tips and applying them in the workplace, as well as in your own life. 


**Happy Friday! We hope you have a safe and healthy weekend!**
