**Taking Time Off**
===================

**Why a break can help you be your best**
-----------------------------------------

**THIS WEEK AT **
-----------------

**A GLANCE**
------------


- This weekend is the Easter break 
- Taking breaks improves focus, motivation, and productivity while reducing stress and preventing burnout 
- The college will be providing limited support during the long weekend

**Dear Learners,**
------------------


💫🐇 Happy Easter Break!


We hope that you all find some way to make the most out of the public holidays, whether that’s doing something relaxing, or doing something exciting. Perhaps spending time with family, friends, or getting some well-deserved alone time, however you best recharge. 


For some people, this time off is a chance to either catch up on things, or to get ahead, but for others, some leisure time is a key strategy in overall productivity. Why might this be?

**Taking a break can have many benefits, including helping you to:**


1. Stay focused by breaking up boredom and exhaustion 
2. Give your brain a chance to rest and recharge 
3. Feel more energised and boosts motivation 
4. Reevaluate goals and make a solid plan of action 
5. Reduce stress and lower the risk of burnout 

With all these great reasons why taking a break increases your wellbeing and productivity in the long run, we all wish you the best with the next few days.


**✨ Happy long weekend! Enjoy your break, and come back refreshed and ready to go. Remember, a bit of self-care goes a long way. Have a great and relaxing weekend!**
