                                          We’re making changes to your Everyday Mastercard®    Dear Cardholder,
                                            
We’re making changes to your Australia Post Everyday Mastercard effective 23 April 2026. Here’s what’s changing and what you can do.
Foreign Currency Wallets Will Be Removed
>From 23 April 2026, foreign currency wallets will no longer be available on the Australia Post Everyday Mastercard, in order to focus on the Everyday nature of the product.
>From that date,  you’ll only be able to load AUD, and rest assured, you can continue to shop instore or online at millions of locations worldwide. Your card will automatically convert the amount from AUD into the local currency at the time of purchase.
If you currently have a foreign currency balance, you can take the following actions before 22 April 2026:•Spend your foreign currency funds before 22 April [11:59pm]: or•Transfer your foreign currency balance to AUD before 22 April [11:59pm] 2026
We will send you a reminder closer to this date.
If a foreign currency balance remains from 23 April 2026, 9:00am AEST, it will be automatically converted to AUD using the applicable exchange rate at the time of conversion. The rates are set in accordance with the PDS. The rates are updated daily. You can continue to use your card with no interruptions.
Reloads via BPAY will be removed
>From 23 April 2026, BPAY will no longer be available as a load option for the Everyday Mastercard to focus on more immediate load methods. You can continue to reload online via ‘My Account’ or the Australia Post Everyday Card app using  bank transfer or debit card load. You can also reload your card at any participating Australia Post Outlets using cash or a debit card.
If you currently use BPAY,  you may consider removing any saved BPAY details from your internet banking before 23 April 2026 as any BPAY reload payments made after this date will no longer be processed.
Changes to fees on the Australia Post Everyday Mastercard from 23 April 2026
The following fee changes will apply from 23 April 2026:FeeChangeCard Purchase FeeAUD $9.95Monthly Management FeeAUD $1.00 per month, charged approximately one month after the card is activated and then monthly on or around the same date.Card Renewal FeeAUD $9.95Card Management Fee – will be removedAUD $2.00 per year
Added purchase protection benefit
>From 23 April 2026, your Everyday Mastercard will include Purchase Protection as an additional benefit.#
Purchase Protection# provides up to 180 consecutive days of free worldwide cover against theft and accidental damage over a wide range of new personal goods purchased anywhere in the world, provided the purchase is charged to the account holder’s Everyday Mastercard.
You do not need to enrol for this benefit; it is automatically activated when you make your first purchase with your Everyday Mastercard. Limits & terms & conditions apply#
For more information about Purchase Protection click here.
Your Everyday Mastercard continues to be protected by Mastercard Zero Liability* Protection for safer shopping, where you are not liable for any unauthorised transactions.
Need help?
For further details and FAQs about these changes click here, or call 1800 549 718 if you have any other questions.
