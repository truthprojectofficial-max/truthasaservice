**Conflict of Interest**
========================

**How to manage it**
--------------------

**THIS WEEK AT **
-----------------

**A GLANCE**
------------


- Conflicts of interest arise when personal factors affect professional judgement 
- All conflicts (including perceived) must be identified and reported 
- Manage conflicts by following organisational processes and seeking guidance

**Dear Learners,**
------------------


Throughout your life, you may come across situations where aspects of your personal life clash with your professional interests, or vice versa, which may influence your ability to perform your role effectively. This is called a conflict of interest.


Conflicts of interest can be caused by personal relationships, political views, financial interests or religious differences. When one of these or other factors interfere with your ability to carry out your duties in a professional manner, then you have a conflict of interest.


Sometimes, you may have a conflict of interest with an individual but refuse to act on it. This is called a perceived conflict of interest, and still needs to be reported as it may make others doubt your ability to perform your role impartially.


It is best to avoid conflicts of interest wherever possible; however, if a conflict of interest cannot be avoided, it must be carefully managed.

**How to manage conflicts of interest in the workplace:**


- **Identify if you have a conflict of interest: **assess the situation and determine if a conflict of interest exists 
- **Declare and report: **the organisation you work at may require you to** **complete a declaration form that states all the relevant information regarding your conflict of interest. This will help your organisation to investigate the matter thoroughly. Submit the form to a supervisor or manager 
- **Follow restrictions: **the organisation will most likely set restrictions based on the extent of the conflict of interest 

During your studies, you may also come across a situation where you will have to report a conflict of interest to the staff at ACCSC. For example, completing work placement under a supervisor who is also a family member or friend. Remember that if you are ever unsure if a conflict of interest exists while you are on work placement, you can ask your trainer.
