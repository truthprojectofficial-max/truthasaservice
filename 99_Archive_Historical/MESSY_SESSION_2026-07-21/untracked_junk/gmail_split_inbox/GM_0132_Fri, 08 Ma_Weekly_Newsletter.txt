**Dear Learners,**
------------------


**National Palliative Care Week 2026** will be held from **10–16 May**, with the theme “**Getting to the heart of it: Big Questions. Real Answers.**” This week encourages all Australians to better understand palliative care and to have open, respectful conversations about care, comfort, dignity and choice.


Palliative care is often misunderstood. It is not only about end-of-life care; it is also about supporting people with life-limiting illnesses to live as well as possible, for as long as possible. It focuses on quality of life, pain and symptom management, emotional wellbeing, cultural needs, family support, and helping people stay connected to what matters most.


As students, you are developing skills that can make a meaningful difference in workplaces and communities. Whether or not your studies include providing palliative care, National Palliative Care Week is a reminder of the importance of compassion, communication, respect and person-centred support across all industries.

This week is also an opportunity to reflect on how we speak about difficult topics. Asking questions, listening without judgement, and showing empathy are powerful skills in both professional and everyday situations.

We encourage you to take a moment during the coming week to learn more, start a conversation, or consider how dignity, choice and care can be supported in your future workplace.


**Thank you for continuing to build the knowledge, confidence and values that help create safer, kinder and more inclusive communities. We hope you have a happy Friday.**
