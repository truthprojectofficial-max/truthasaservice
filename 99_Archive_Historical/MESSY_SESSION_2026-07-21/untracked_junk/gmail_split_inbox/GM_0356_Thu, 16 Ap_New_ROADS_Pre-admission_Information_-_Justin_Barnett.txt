Pre-Admission Steps:

  *   Ensure that you have the admission fee of $250 in cash ready for the day of your admission.
  *   Ensure you have packed up to two reasonably sized bags of belongings.
  *   Organize any ID and bank cards needed to bring into the program.
  *   Note, ongoing payments will be between $420-$520, deducted fortnightly from your Centrelink.
  *   Please organise to have all medication be distributed into a Webster Pack (You will need to see your GP to access your prescriptions and have them provide a letter to us listing all relevant medication. Webster Packs can be organised at any chemist for a cost of approximately $7).
  *   A requirement of your preadmission is to attend a meeting (DATE) with myself to begin preadmission paperwork, and go over the program, this includes a safety plan. Non-attendance may result in your offer being withdrawn. As discussed, I encourage you to reach out to New Roads if you encounter any barriers.
  *   Attend DASSA detox prior to your New Roads admission.
  *
Purchase your bus ticket to Whyalla and present there for admission. https://stateliner.com.au/ a link to the bus website. Make sure you book the morning bus.



DASSA Withdrawal Admission Advice:

  *   DASSA WSG is smoke free and nicotine replacement will be provided
  *   DASSA WSG is a closed unit, so no leave and no personal visitors allowed except for case workers
  *   Mobile phones allowed into unit, but no photos or videos can be taken
  *   All property will be searched for alcohol or drugs. Illicit drugs, weapons and dangerous items will need to be discarded and cannot be kept by the unit
  *   Do not bring excess money or luggage. Excess belongings will be placed in the storeroom and returned on discharge.  We do have a washing machine
  *   Any drinks, snacks or food items must be in sealed unopened packaging and nothing homemade
  *   Do not drive to detox, please arrange other transport
  *   No aerosols or perfumes can be used inside the unit, roll on deodorant only
  *   Bring all prescribed medication or prescriptions with you
  *   Bring Medicare and Health Care cards if you have them
  *   Arrive at the appointed time as late arrivals might not be admitted unless you have rung to tell us what time you will be here and check that arriving late is ok, you can also rebook by calling 7087 1700
  *   Stop all AOD use the night before admission
  *   Stop alcohol and other drugs at 6pm as you may not be admitted if excessively intoxicated
  *   Stop alcohol at 24:00 if you have a history of seizures.
  *   Do not cease if using GHB


Day of Admission:

  *
Present at the bus station on the day of admission, we can discuss the best way for you to get to the bus from DASSA. In Whyalla you will then be transported to your allocated house by New Roads staff.
  *   Additional drug and alcohol screens will be completed at random times during your first week into the Residential program.


CENTRELINK & JOBSEEKER EXEMPTION:

  1.  If you are accessing Jobseeker, please see your GP to secure an exemption prior to entry.

HOUSING SA

You will need ID to do this.
  *   On the day of admission, you will attend a Housing SA office to complete the Owners Declaration Form. At this time, you will receive the outcome of your initial application form and your cheque (if approved for financial assistance).
  *   Please ensure you have 100 points of ID to complete this form. Appropriate ID can be found on the Housing SA site at Proof of income, identity and rent | SA Housing Authority<https://www.housing.sa.gov.au/about-us/policies/proof-of-income,-identity-and-rent>
