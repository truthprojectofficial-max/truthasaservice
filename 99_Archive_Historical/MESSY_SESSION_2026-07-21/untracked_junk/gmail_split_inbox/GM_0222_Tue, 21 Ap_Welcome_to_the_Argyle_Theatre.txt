A Message from the Artistic Director: Margie Argyle
----------------------------------------------------

Sorry to state the obvious, but I’m Margie Argyle and my
official role at the Argyle Theatre is artistic director, but I
deplore the constraints of middle management jargon. I prefer to
be seen as a story teller, disruptor, truth custodian, and vile
feminist witch (come at me).

The 2026 theatre season at the Argyle is destined to stun you,
to challenge you, to uplift you and to break you down so you’re
left weeping in the aisles and screaming for your father.

I love my life, I love my job, I love the theatre and despite
the aggressive capitalist and patriarchal confines in which I
continue to persevere as an artist, the Argyle Theatre will
continue to tread new ground and reject the mainstream. You will
never see a production of ‘Grease’ on our stage as long as I live
(in this life or any other incarnation).

Love, Margie.

A message from the CEO: Julia McNamara
--------------------------------------

I’m extremely proud of what we’ve managed to cobble together
for our 2026 program after a series of unprofessional outbursts
by people who will remain nameless. Artistic vision has been
counter balanced with more realistic, financially driven
decisions that will attract audiences from all walks of life and
not just people over 85 with architect designed holiday homes.

I’m not going to lie to you, there have been immense challenges
to overcome and at times, the more I learnt about this industry,
the more daunting the role seemed.

Whilst my position here as CEO was somewhat unexpected, I pride
myself on getting results, and this season promises to be our
best and most profitable yet. So get your Sandy and Danny
costumes ready because ‘Grease’ is back!

See you at the Argyle,

Julia McNamara
